## Overview

**LocaLeJEPA** keeps LeJEPA’s agreement across views while replacing its global Gaussian constraint with **LocaLeReg**. The regulariser forms neighbourhoods in projected embeddings, centres each neighbourhood separately, and matches its residual distribution to a covariance-normalised uniform ball. Local spreading discourages collapse while leaving neighbourhood locations free to organise.

A heat-flow perspective motivates the construction. Frozen-feature experiments evaluate recognition, transfer and controlled rotation recovery across Euclidean, hyperbolic and equivariant encoders.

Kartik Tandon and Alessio Borgi contributed equally.
